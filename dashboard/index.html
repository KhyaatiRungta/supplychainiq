Supply chain performance, measured not asserted.

A read-only snapshot of delivery reliability, shipping behaviour and profitability across 65,752 distinct orders from January 2015 to January 2018. Every figure is computed from the source data; no correlation is claimed as causation.

Data source: DataCo SMART Supply Chain for Big Data Analysis (Kaggle / Mendeley). General retail/e-commerce supply chain, not MSD data and not pharmaceutical shipments.
